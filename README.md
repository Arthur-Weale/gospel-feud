# Gospel Feud 🎮📖

**Gospel Feud** is a fun, interactive scripture-based game inspired by *Family Feud*, designed to help participants learn, reflect, and engage with the teachings of Jesus Christ.

## 🎯 About the Project

This game was created for a Gospel lesson focused on **Isaiah 58**, exploring themes such as true fasting, compassion, caring for those in need, and keeping the Sabbath day holy.

The goal is to turn scripture study into an engaging group activity that encourages teamwork, participation, and meaningful discussion.

## 📖 Scripture Focus

**Isaiah 58 — True Fasting and the Sabbath**

Key themes include:
- The purpose and meaning of true fasting.
- Helping the hungry and caring for those in need.
- Showing kindness and compassion to others.
- Honouring the Sabbath day.
- Recognizing the blessings associated with righteous living.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Divide participants into teams.
2. Present questions inspired by Isaiah 58 and Gospel principles.
3. Let teams discuss their answers and take turns responding.
4. Award points for correct answers according to the game's rules.
5. Use the questions to encourage further scripture discussion and personal application.

*Gameplay rules and features may evolve as development continues.*

## 🎯 Purpose

To make Gospel learning more engaging, strengthen understanding of the scriptures, and encourage participants to apply Gospel principles in their daily lives.
